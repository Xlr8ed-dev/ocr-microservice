TENDER FOR SUPPLY OF INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this tender is to invite qualified suppliers to submit proposals for the supply of industrial equipment as per the specification provided in Annexure A.

1.2 Scope of Work

The scope includes supply, installation, and commissioning of the equipment. The vendor shall also provide one year of on-site support.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

2.1 Minimum Turnover

The bidder must have an average annual turnover of at least INR 5 crore over the last three financial years.

2.2 Past Experience

The bidder must have executed at least two similar contracts in the last five years.

3. SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

3.1 Format

All submissions must be in PDF format and submitted through the online portal.

3.2 Deadline

The deadline for submission is 15th November 2026, 17:00 IST.
